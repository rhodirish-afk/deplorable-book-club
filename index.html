The Deplorable Book Club

Books the trade will not touch.
A club that will.

We exist for the unpublishables — sharp history, awkward biography, cultural critique that never clears the risk committee — and for the deplorables who still want to read them. Editing and design, Amazon KDP, a monthly essay magazine, forums, and UK author nights. Not a bland book box.

Why bother

Soft censorship is still censorship

Mainstream lists favour safe taste. Historians, journalists, and thinkers with heterodox ideas — forgotten wars, contested culture, inconvenient biography — often find the door shut. We open another one, without pretending every provocation is wisdom.

We sell the authors as hard as the books: profiles, events, and shared marketing so solo writers are not left shouting into the void. Readers get unfiltered work; authors get craft help and a room that actually wants the argument.
