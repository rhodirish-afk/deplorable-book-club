Privacy

This is a public marketing site for The Deplorable Book Club, hosted on GitHub Pages. Ordinary static HTML — no accounts, no checkout, no cookie wall.

What we do not do (yet). There is no membership login, payment processor, or analytics suite on this domain. When the subscription app and shop launch, this page will be rewritten properly.

Waitlist and contact. If you email myone_ie@hotmail.co.uk, that correspondence lives in ordinary email. Do not send sensitive personal data you would not want in an inbox.

Hosting. GitHub and CDNs (including the Tailwind CDN) may process standard technical request logs. See their own privacy documentation.

External links. Links to Amazon, newspapers, or social platforms are governed by those sites’ policies, not ours.

Questions: myone_ie@hotmail.co.uk.
